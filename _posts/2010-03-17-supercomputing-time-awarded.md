title: Supercomputing time awarded to the group
excerpt: The team received major computing allocations from the MareNostrum Consortium and Portugal's Milipeia cluster.
archive: true
---

The group received one million CPU hours through Spain's MareNostrum Consortium, with Vitor Cardoso as principal investigator, and a further 120,000 CPU hours on Portugal's Milipeia cluster, with Helvi Witek as principal investigator.
