---
title: Computing time awarded at ICTS-CESGA
excerpt: Collaborators received 500,000 CPU hours for binary-black-hole simulations on Spain's FinisTerrae cluster.
archive: true
---

Carlos Sopuerta, Ulrich Sperhake, and Priscilla Cañizares received 500,000 CPU hours for *Simulations of Binary Black Hole Systems as Sources of Gravitational Radiation*. The allocation provided access to the FinisTerrae supercomputer at ICTS-CESGA in Spain.
