Group research on “floating orbits” around rotating black holes was published in *Physical Review Letters*. The work explores how an orbiting body can exchange energy with a rotating black hole through a massive scalar field.

Read the [paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.6021) or the [Italian-language outreach article from INAF](https://www.media.inaf.it/2011/12/16/galleggiando-sulle-onde-gravitazionali/).
