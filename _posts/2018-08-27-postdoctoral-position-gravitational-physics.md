---
title: Postdoctoral search in gravitational physics
excerpt: The gravity group opened a postdoctoral search in strong-field gravity, compact objects, and gravitational-wave science.
---

In August 2018, the University of Mississippi gravity group invited applications for a postdoctoral position in gravitational physics and astrophysics. The search covered strong-field gravity, compact objects, gravitational waves, tests of general relativity, numerical relativity, and gravitational-wave data analysis.

This is a historical announcement and the search is closed.
