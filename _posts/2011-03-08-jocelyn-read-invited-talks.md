Jocelyn Read was invited to present at the 2011 APS April Meeting in Anaheim in a session on neutron-star matter. She also received support to participate in the APS Professional Skills Development Workshop.

Further invitations included *Exploring Physics with Neutron Stars* in Tucson and the Gravitational-Wave Physics and Astronomy Workshop in Milwaukee.
