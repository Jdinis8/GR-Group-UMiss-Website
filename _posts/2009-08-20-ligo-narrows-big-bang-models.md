title: LIGO observations narrow models of the Big Bang
excerpt: University of Mississippi physicists contributed to research that placed new limits on primordial gravitational waves.

---

University of Mississippi physicists participated in LIGO research that placed new limits on gravitational waves from the early Universe, constraining some models of the Big Bang.

Read the contemporary coverage from [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2009/ligo-0820) or the [original Ole Miss News story](http://news.olemiss.edu/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=4615:ligo-results09&catid=108:liberalarts&Itemid=12).
