In March 2014, [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/140305-black-hole-spin-science-galaxies-space) spoke with Emanuele Berti about the measurement of the spin of a supermassive black hole in a lensed quasar at redshift \(z = 0.658\).

The original result was published in [Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13031).
