---
title: Resonant shattering of neutron-star crusts
excerpt: Jocelyn Read's work on neutron-star crusts was selected as a Physical Review Letters Editors' Suggestion and covered widely in the press.
archive: true
---

Jocelyn Read and collaborators from Caltech and Penn State studied how tidal resonances could shatter a neutron star's crust shortly before a collision. Their [Physical Review Letters paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.011102) was selected as an Editors' Suggestion.

The result was featured in an [APS Physics Synopsis](https://physics.aps.org/synopsis-for/10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.011102), [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/science/2012/01/some-neutron-stars-might-shed-their-skins/), and [New Scientist](https://www.newscientist.com/article/dn21356-astrophile-glimpse-elusive-matter-in-shattering-star.html).
