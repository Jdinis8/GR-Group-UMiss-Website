Emanuele Berti commented on new measurements suggesting that the masses of some supermassive black holes may have been overestimated.

The research appeared in [Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature09761) and was discussed by [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/observations/some-supermassive-black-holes-may-not-be-so-super-after-all/) and *Cosmos* magazine.
