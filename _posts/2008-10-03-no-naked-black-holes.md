title: No naked black holes
excerpt: High-energy black-hole collision simulations showed that head-on encounters radiate about 14 percent of their center-of-mass energy.
archive: true
---

Group results on high-energy black-hole collisions showed that head-on encounters radiate about 14 percent of their center-of-mass energy while preserving the horizons that hide their singularities.

The work was covered by [Science News](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/no-naked-black-holes).
