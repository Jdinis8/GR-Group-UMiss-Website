University publications reported on Emanuele Berti's NSF CAREER Award for black-hole research. The award and the work it supported were featured by [Ole Miss Z!NG](http://zing.olemiss.edu/um-physicist-honored-for-black-hole-research/) and in the College of Liberal Arts newsletter, [The View from Ventress](http://viewfromventress.org/newsletter/summer-2011-newsletter/college-faculty-recognized-with-career-awards/).
