In July 2013, [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/big-bang-light-reveals-lifetime-photon/) spoke with Emanuele Berti about research using the cosmic microwave background to place a lower limit on the lifetime of the photon.

The story was also covered by [Nature](https://www.nature.com/news/big-bang-light-reveals-minimum-lifetime-of-photons-1.13474). The paper discussed in both articles is available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.2821).
