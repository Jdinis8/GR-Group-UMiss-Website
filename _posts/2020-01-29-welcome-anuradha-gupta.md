Anuradha Gupta joined the University of Mississippi gravity group as an assistant professor in January 2020.

She received her PhD from the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research in Mumbai and was previously a postdoctoral researcher at Penn State's Institute for Gravitation and the Cosmos. Her research spans gravitational-wave modeling, detection and parameter estimation, spinning and precessing binaries, binary formation, neutron-star structure, tests of gravity, numerical relativity, and detector data characterization.
