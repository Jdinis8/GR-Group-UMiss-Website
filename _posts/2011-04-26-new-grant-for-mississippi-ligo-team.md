---
title: New NSF grant for the Mississippi LIGO team
excerpt: The Mississippi LIGO team received NSF support to prepare for the Advanced LIGO era.
archive: true
---

The Mississippi LIGO team received a three-year National Science Foundation grant, *Mississippi Participation in LIGO's Search for Gravitational Waves: Getting Ready for Advanced LIGO*. The award was scheduled to begin on July 1, 2011.
