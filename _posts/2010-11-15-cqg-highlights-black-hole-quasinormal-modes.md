*Quasinormal Modes of Black Holes and Black Branes*, a review by Emanuele Berti, Vitor Cardoso, and Andrei Starinets, was selected as one of the 2009–2010 highlights of *Classical and Quantum Gravity*.

The review is available from the [journal](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/26/16/163001) and [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.2975).
