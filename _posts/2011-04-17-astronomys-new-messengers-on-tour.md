---
title: Astronomy's New Messengers goes on tour
excerpt: The group's gravitational-wave outreach exhibit returned at Shawnee State University and the University of Texas at Arlington.
archive: true
---

*Astronomy's New Messengers: Listening to the Universe with Gravitational Waves*, the public outreach project directed by Marco Cavaglià, went on display again in spring 2011. The full LIGO exhibit traveled to Shawnee State University in Ohio, while a portable version was displayed at the University of Texas at Arlington.
