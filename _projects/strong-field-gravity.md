---
title: Strong-field gravity
number: "03"
intro: Testing Einstein’s theory around black holes and neutron stars, where gravity is nonlinear, dynamical, and strongest.
---
The strong gravitational fields around black holes and neutron stars let us
test general relativity in nonlinear and dynamical regimes. We develop
theoretical predictions and observational tests that distinguish general
relativity from modified theories and from compact objects that can mimic
black-hole signals.

## Methods

Our work draws on perturbation theory, numerical relativity, waveform
modeling, and statistical inference. We study black-hole dynamics and
structure, possible deviations from Einstein gravity, and the multipolar and
ringdown information encoded in gravitational radiation.
