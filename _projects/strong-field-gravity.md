title: Strong-field gravity
number: "02"
intro: Testing Einstein’s theory in the nonlinear, dynamical regime where gravity is strongest.
---
Merging compact objects create laboratories for gravity that cannot be reproduced on Earth. We develop theoretical predictions and observational tests designed to distinguish general relativity from possible alternatives.

## Methods

Our work draws on perturbation theory, numerical relativity, waveform modeling, and statistical inference to connect fundamental theory with measurable signals.
