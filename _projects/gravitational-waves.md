Gravitational waves let us observe systems that can be dark to conventional telescopes. Our work connects precise source models to detector data, helping extract the masses, spins, dynamics, and environments of compact objects.

## Questions we pursue

- How accurately can we model merging black holes and neutron stars?
- What do gravitational-wave signals tell us about strong-field gravity?
- How can detector data reveal populations of compact objects across cosmic history?
