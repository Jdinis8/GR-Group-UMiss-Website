Symmetry, locality, causality, and unitarity place powerful restrictions on
physical theories. We use these principles to explore gravitational physics
without committing to a particular microscopic model.

## Methods and applications

Our work develops effective field theories, scattering amplitudes, positivity
bounds, cosmological correlators, and conformal-bootstrap techniques. We also
apply bootstrap ideas to spectral geometry, linking properties of manifolds
and compactifications to the spectra and interactions of physical theories.
