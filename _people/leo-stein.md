Leo studies Einstein’s theory of gravity through astrophysical observations.
He analytically models and numerically simulates black-hole systems as they
spiral together, merge, and emit gravitational waves. Predictions from general
relativity and theories beyond it provide ways to test Einstein’s theory with
observations.
