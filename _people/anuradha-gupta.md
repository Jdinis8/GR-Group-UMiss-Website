Anuradha studies gravitational-wave physics and astrophysics, with an emphasis
on detecting signals and estimating the properties of binary systems made of
black holes and neutron stars. She uses these observations to test general
relativity and investigate questions in cosmology. She is a member of the LIGO
Scientific Collaboration and the LISA Consortium.
