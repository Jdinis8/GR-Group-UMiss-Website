Daniel Nicholson is a Master’s student working with advisor Dr. Anuradha Gupta. His research investigates potential deviations of General Relativity that can be observed in gravitational wave observations. He is passionate about contributing to the emerging field of gravitational wave astronomy as well as public outreach and science communication.
